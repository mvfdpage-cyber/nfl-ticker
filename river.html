Reading the river

Gage height is the water surface above that gauge’s fixed reference point. It is not river depth, and feet at one station should not be compared directly with feet at another. Flood stages and operating levels are specific to each gauge. Each card shows the station’s own timestamp and category when provided by NOAA.

Data & update cadence

Observed readings and any available official NWS flood context come from NOAA’s National Water Prediction Service. NOAA observations may arrive at different times by station. The monitor checks hourly while open; use the station link for the official live page.
